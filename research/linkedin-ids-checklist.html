LinkedIn company ids

Round 2: the 12 new companies, plus Samsung. For the "Find your connections" button. With a verified id the button finds people currently at the company; without one it falls back to a name search.

  1. Candidate given (none this round): click check. If LinkedIn opens that company's page, tick "correct". If it opens another company, tick "wrong".
  2. No candidate (or wrong): click find, open the company's main page (most followers, right logo), click See all N employees, copy the whole address from the address bar and paste it into the box. The number is picked out for you (the green line shows it). A "See all jobs" address (f_C=) works too.
  3. Company hiring under several pages (e.g. Samsung Electronics + Samsung R&D Institute Bangalore / Noida): paste each page's address (or number) into the same box, separated by spaces. All of them are kept and the button searches them together.
  4. Press Copy results and paste the text into the chat.
